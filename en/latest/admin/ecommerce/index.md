# E-Commerce

The E-Commerce section covers the complete sales workflow of your store: listing products, accepting payments through checkout, and fulfilling incoming orders. Physical and digital products alike support variations, inventory tracking, and tax rules.

Beyond day-to-day selling, you can boost revenue with coupons, recover lost sales through abandoned cart emails, offer subscriptions and donations, publish your catalog to marketplaces via product feeds, and give customers a self-service account area.

## What You Can Do

- **🛒 Products**: Sell physical and digital products with variations, inventory tracking, pricing, and taxes.
- **💳 Checkout & Payments**: Accept one-time payments and subscriptions through multiple payment gateways.
- **📦 Orders**: Manage incoming orders and keep customers informed.
- **🎟️ Coupons**: Create percentage or fixed discounts for the whole store or specific categories.
- **📤 Product Feeds**: Export your catalog to marketplaces like Shopify, Idealo, or Yandex Market.
- **👤 Customer Account**: Let customers manage their orders and subscriptions.
- **🛒 Abandoned Cart Recovery**: Recover lost sales automatically.
- **🗓️ Subscriptions**: Donations, recurring billing, and subscription-based plans.
