# Comments

Engage with your audience by enabling comments on your posts and products.

Vale supports four comment systems: **Local** (built-in), **Disqus**, **Giscus** (GitHub Discussions), and **Facebook Comments**. Choose your platform in [Discussion Settings](../settings/discussion#comment-system).

## Moderation

All comments can be managed from the **Comments** panel. You can:

- **Edit**: Open a modal to fix a comment's author, email, content or status.
- **Approve**: Make the comment visible to the public.
- **Unapprove**: Send approved comments back to Pending (bulk action).
- **Mark as Spam**: Hide the comment and move it to the **Spam** tab (restore it via **Approve** there or with bulk actions). Spam comments are not auto-deleted; you can remove them in bulk in **[Settings](../settings/) → [Maintenance](../settings/maintenance#cleanup)**.
- **Delete**: Permanently remove the comment.

Tabs at the top filter comments by status (**All**, **Pending**, **Approved**, **Spam**). The type filter lists every content type with comments enabled; product **Reviews** appear there too when the shop and product reviews are active. Replies are flagged with a "Reply to" line pointing at the parent comment.

## Settings

You can control comment behavior globally or per-content:

- **Global**: Enable or disable comments for the entire site, hold guest comments for moderation, require login, choose the comment system and configure threaded replies (up to a **Maximum reply depth** of 1–10 levels) in **[Discussion Settings](../settings/discussion)**.
- **Per-Content**: Override the site-wide setting for individual posts in the editor's Comments field (**Use global / Enabled / Disabled**).
