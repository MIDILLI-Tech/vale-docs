# Comments

Engage with your audience by enabling comments on your posts and products.

Vale supports four comment systems: **Local** (built-in), **Disqus**, **Giscus** (GitHub Discussions), and **Facebook Comments**. Choose your platform in [Discussion Settings](../settings/discussion#comment-system).

## Reply Threading

When nested comments are enabled, users can reply to each other in a threaded format:

- Replies are indented up to 3 visual levels.
- Beyond that, replies flatten and display a **"Replying to @name"** label.
- The maximum reply depth is configurable in Discussion Settings (1–10 levels).
- Each comment has a direct anchor link (e.g., `#comment-42`) so you can share or link to a specific comment.

## Moderation

All comments can be managed from the **Comments** panel. You can:
- **Approve**: Make the comment visible to the public.
- **Reply**: Respond directly to a customer or reader.
- **Mark as Spam**: Hide the comment and help train the spam filter.
- **Delete**: Permanently remove the comment.

## Settings

You can control comment behavior globally or per-post:
- **Global**: Enable or disable comments for the entire site.
- **Per-Content**: Override global settings for specific posts or products.
- **Nested Comments**: Allow users to reply to each other in a threaded format.
