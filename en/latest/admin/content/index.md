# Content Management

The Content section is where everything your visitors read comes together — blog posts, static pages, custom content types, media files, and reader comments. Whether you are writing a quick announcement or building a structured portfolio with its own fields, this is the place to start.

A modern Vale Editor with content blocks and embeds keeps the writing experience smooth, while revisions preserve every change so you can always restore an earlier version. Content can also be translated into multiple languages from a single interface.

## What You Can Do

- **📝 Blog**: Publish posts, updates, and storytelling content.
- **📄 Pages**: Create landing pages and static content like About or Contact.
- **🗂️ Custom Content Types**: Define custom post types like portfolio, recipes, or testimonials with their own fields.
- **🌍 Multi-Language Content**: Translate posts, pages, and more into multiple languages.
- **✍️ Modern Editor**: Write and format content with the built-in Vale Editor, including content blocks, embeds, and media.
- **📚 Multi-Page Content**: Split long posts into pages with the Next Page block (see [Editor](./editor#next-page)).
- **🗑️ Revisions**: Track changes to posts and pages and restore previous versions.
- **🖼️ Media**: Upload, organize, and reuse images and files across your content.
- **💬 Comments**: Moderate reader comments and control discussion settings.
