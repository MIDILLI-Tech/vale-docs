# Marketing

The Marketing section brings the tools that grow and understand your audience into one place: newsletters, visual automations, analytics, and advertising. Collect subscribers, send campaigns without a third-party email service, and build automated follow-up flows with a drag-and-drop editor.

While campaigns bring visitors in, built-in analytics shows you what they do — page views, referrers, and visitor statistics tracked directly on your own server.

## What You Can Do

- **📰 Newsletter**: Collect emails and send announcements to subscribers.
- **⚙️ Automations**: Build visual automation flows for the newsletter, e-commerce, and more.
- **📊 Analytics**: Track page views, referrers, and visitor statistics.
- **📢 Advertising**: Enable advertisement slots and shortcodes for platforms like AdSense or Bing Ads.
